KRA / KPI — Client Guide

Send in your requirements, approve who works on them, and sign off finished work.

About Your Client Portal

This app is how you and your development team stay on the same page. You use it to do three things:

  • Send in work — new requirements, changes to existing work, and bugs you have found.
  • Approve who does it — when the team assigns someone to your task, you get to agree before they begin.
  • Sign off what is delivered — you confirm the work is done and works, and only then can it be invoiced.

You only ever see your own work. Everything is scoped to your account.

IMPORTANT  Nothing is billed until you sign it off. Approving a developer at the start only lets them begin working — it is not approval to invoice. The invoice question is settled at the end, by your sign-off.

How Your Work Flows

  • You upload a requirement, an update or a bug report
  • The team turns it into tasks and assigns a developer
  • You approve or reject the assignment
  • The developer does the work
  • An admin checks it, then sends it to you
  • You sign it off — and only now can it appear on an invoice

PART 1   GETTING INTO THE APP

Step 1   Open the App

Open the KRA KPI app on your phone. After a short animation you reach a welcome card. Tap Tap to Enter Login Page.

Step 2   Enter Your Mobile Number

Your country dial code is already filled in. Type the rest of your mobile number and tap Continue. Use the number your account was set up with.

Step 3   Enter Your Password

Type your password and tap Log In.

The very first time, your password is 1111. The app shows a red warning saying so. Please change it straight away — tap Forgot password?.

Step 4   Set Your Own Password

A 4-digit code is sent to you on WhatsApp. Type the code, type your new password twice, then tap Verify & Set Password. If the code does not arrive, tap Resend code.

You can change your password any time later from the Profile tab, the same way.

Step 7   Add Your Content

Section 2 Source & Bulk Import gives you three ways to get your work in. Use whichever suits you:

  • Paste text — paste your notes into the box marked “Paste requirement / update / bug text…”. The app reads it and suggests a list of tasks.
  • Import a spreadsheet — tap Choose file and pick an Excel (.xlsx / .xls) or CSV file. If you pick anything else the app tells you: “Please choose an Excel (.xlsx/.xls) or CSV (.csv) file.”
  • Download Blank Template — get a correctly formatted spreadsheet to fill in first. This is the easiest route for a long list.
TIP  Pasting plain text is the quickest way to raise a handful of items. For a big batch, download the blank template, fill it in on a computer, and import it.

Step 8   Check and Edit the Task Rows

Section 3 lists the tasks that will be created. Every row can be edited, and you can add your own with Add Row. For each row you can set:

PriorityRegular, Important or Urgent.

Section 4 Defaults for new rows lets you set the hours, minutes and priority that every new row starts with — handy when you are adding many similar items.

Use Add attachment for documents, or Add photos / video for screen recordings and screenshots. This is especially useful for bug reports.

The app asks Why are you attaching this? — type a short reason, and pick which task it belongs to. Both are required, so the team knows what they are looking at.

TIP  For a bug, a short screen recording showing the problem is worth several paragraphs of description.

When the rows look right, tap Create All Tasks. The app confirms how many were created.

If any row fails, it is listed and a Retry failed button appears — tap it to re-send only the ones that did not go through. Reset clears the form and starts again.

PART 4   APPROVING WHO DOES THE WORK

Step 11   Open Requests

Tap Requests. Anything needing you is pulled to the top in two sections:

  • ✋ Approval needed — a developer has been assigned and is waiting on your go-ahead.
  • ⚠ Awaiting your sign-off — work that is finished and needs you to confirm it.

Below those sits your full task list.

IMAGE 10

Requests screen — the “Approval needed” and “Awaiting your sign-off” sections

Step 12   Approve or Reject the Developer

Each card under Approval needed shows the task and who has been assigned to it, with a countdown.

  • Tap Approve if you are happy. You will see “Approved — the developer can start.”
  • Tap Reject if you are not, and choose a reason.
ReasonUse it when
🔁 Want a different developerYou would rather someone else did this.
📝 Task details are wrongThe task has been written up incorrectly.
🚫 Not needed anymoreYou no longer want this done.
✍ OtherAnything else. You must type an explanation — the team cannot act on a blank reason.

After rejecting you will see “Rejected — sent back to the team.”

IMAGE 11

The Reject reason picker with its four choices

Step 13   Stop the Clock with Hold

Not ready to decide? Tap Hold. The countdown stops and nothing auto-approves while it is held. Approve and Reject still work at any time. Tap Resume to restart the countdown.

IMPORTANT  If you do nothing, the approval releases on its own when the countdown runs out, so your work is never stuck waiting on us. That is only permission to start work — it is never permission to invoice. You still sign off at the end, and that is what makes a task billable.

PART 5   SIGNING OFF FINISHED WORK

Step 14   Open the Sign-off

When work is delivered it appears under ⚠ Awaiting your sign-off. Review it, then tap Sign off this task.

Step 15   Confirm and Sign

The sign-off panel asks you to confirm three things. Tick each one:

  • The work was delivered to me
  • I have checked it and it works
  • I'm authorized to approve this

Then under Type your name to sign, type your full name and submit.

The app notes: “This records your digital approval and generates the completion certificate.”

IMAGE 12

The “Sign off this task” panel — three tick boxes and the name field

IMPORTANT  Only tick these if they are true. Your sign-off is what turns the task Completed and makes it billable. If the work is not right, do not sign — contact your coordinator and it will be sent back to the team.

PART 8   NOTIFICATIONS AND YOUR PROFILE

Notifications

Tap the bell on Home. Three tabs sort everything:

  • All — everything.
  • Unread — what you have not opened yet.
  • Action needed — the ones waiting on you. Check this one first.

Items are grouped into Today, Yesterday and Earlier, and anything critical carries a red URGENT badge. Tap a notification to jump to the task or invoice it is about. Mark all read clears the badge.

You will be notified when a task needs your approval, when work is ready for your sign-off, and when an invoice is issued to you.

Things to Remember

  • Your sign-off is the only thing that makes work billable. Auto-approval at the start never bills you for anything.
  • Check the Action needed tab on your notifications — it is the shortest list of things only you can do.
  • Put one item per task row. Two requirements in one row become one task and are harder to track.
  • For bugs, attach a screenshot or a short screen recording. It saves a round of questions.
  • Use Hold if you need time to think about an assignment — it stops the countdown.
  • Rejecting with Other requires an explanation. Be specific and the fix comes back faster.
  • If a list looks out of date, pull down to refresh it.
  • Change your password away from 1111 the first time you log in.
Upload it, approve it, sign it off. Those three steps are your whole job in this app.
